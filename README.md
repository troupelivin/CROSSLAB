# CROSSLAB

**A crosshair overlay for PC games.** Design your crosshair, animate it, and it sits on top of your game.

## Download

**[Download the latest version](https://github.com/troupelivin/CROSSLAB/releases/latest)**: pick `CROSSLAB-Setup-x.y.z.exe` (recommended: installs, adds a Start menu entry and updates itself) or `CROSSLAB-x.y.z-portable.zip` (no install: unzip and run `CROSSLAB.exe`).

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit.

> **"Windows protected your PC"?** CROSSLAB isn't code-signed yet, so SmartScreen may warn the first time. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

## What it does

- Six classic styles, or build your own from pieces in the Creator Studio
- 12 animations, including pops on every shot
- A library of 160+ crosshairs, favourites and collections
- Share crosshairs as codes with friends
- Real-size preview on your own screenshots
- Streamer mode and themes
- Checks for updates when it starts and updates in one click

## Good to know

- Works over games in **Borderless** or **Windowed** mode (exclusive full-screen games draw over everything).
- CROSSLAB never reads or changes your games. It only draws a picture on top of your screen.
- Your crosshairs and settings are saved in `%USERPROFILE%\CROSSLAB` and are kept when you uninstall.
