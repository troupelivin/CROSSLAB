# CROSSLAB patch notes

Newest first. Each "## version" section is what the app shows under "What's new" and what the
GitHub release page shows for that version.

## 1.0.0 - 2026-11-22

The first public release.

- **Crosshair editor:** six styles, any colour, size, thickness, gap, opacity, rotation, outline and a glow you can turn up or down.
- **Creator Studio:** build your own crosshair from pieces (lines, dots, rings, arcs, boxes, diamonds, triangles, chevrons, corners, stars, polygons and icons). Drag them on a zoomed canvas, mirror them, snap to pixels, undo and redo anything.
- **Random, Remix and Describe:** roll a new design (lock the parts you like), mix two crosshairs part by part, or describe one in words.
- **12 animations:** Pulse, Expand, Recoil, Shoot, Hit, Rotate, Breathe, Sway, Bounce, Flicker, Colour shift and Fade (two at a time). Shoot and Hit pop on every left click in game.
- **Library:** 50 hand-picked designs plus 118 community-style crosshairs, with search, favourites and your own collections.
- **Share codes:** copy any crosshair as a code and paste one from a friend.
- **Profiles:** keep several setups and switch with a hotkey.
- **Real-size preview:** compare up to four crosshairs on your own game screenshot and save the picture.
- **Streamer mode:** hide your name and folders, hide CROSSLAB from your stream, and a crosshair card window for OBS.
- **Themes:** 13 looks for the app, from the original crimson to pictures and plain colours.
- **Updates:** CROSSLAB checks for new versions when it starts and updates in one click.
